Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

  • No personal data collected
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • No account or sign-in
  • Progress stays on your device

This policy explains how Mill Road, a mobile game for iOS and Android, handles personal data. In short: the game does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. Mill Road:

  • does not require an account, sign-in or registration,
  • does not read your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers,
  • shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools,
  • contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs),
  • does not need an internet connection to play and sends no data to our servers.

Information stored on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game stores a few settings only on your own device, such as the level you have reached, your sound and language choices, and the points you have earned. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes it.

App stores

When you download the game from the Apple App Store or Google Play, Apple and Google may process information such as downloads, purchases and (if you agreed) crash reports under their own privacy policies. This is outside our control. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Support form

If you contact us through the form on the support page, the information you enter (name, email address, device model and your message) is delivered to us through Microsoft Forms and processed under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We use it only to reply to you, never share it, and delete it on request. The form is outside the game; the game itself collects none of this information.

Children

Mill Road is suitable for players of all ages. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Because the game does not process your personal data, we hold no game records about you to access, correct or delete. If you wrote to us through the support form, you can ask us to show or delete those messages through the same form.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a feature that collects data is ever added to the game, this page will be updated before that.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact form on the support page.
