Mill Road

Send the carts out in the right order and get every sack to the mill.

  • No ads

How to play

  1. Tap a cartIf its way is clear, it drives to the loading bay. If not, it honks: move the carts in front first.
  2. Match the colorsEach cart picks up only sacks of its own color from the bottom row of the belt.
  3. Mind the bayThe loading bay has 3 spots, and points can open 2 more. Fill it in the wrong order and the road jams.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear icon at the top right, then Language. The game supports 10 languages and starts in your device language when it is one of them.

Why was my progress reset?

Progress is stored only on your device. If you delete and reinstall the game or move to a new device, you start again from level 1.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Mill Road plays fully offline and never sends your data anywhere.

Where do I find the game version?

At the bottom of the Settings screen (gear icon). Adding it to your message helps us find problems faster.
